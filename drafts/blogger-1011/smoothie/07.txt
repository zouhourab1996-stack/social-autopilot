<li><strong>Build balanced smoothies:</strong> a protein source, vegetables (spinach, cucumber), one portion of fruit, and some fiber (oats, chia).</li>
<li><strong>Keep at least one solid meal a day</strong> with vegetables and protein.</li>
<li><strong>Plan the exit.</strong> Decide in advance how you will eat after day 21 so the weight does not come straight back.</li>
</ol>
