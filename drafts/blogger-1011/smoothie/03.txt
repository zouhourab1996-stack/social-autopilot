<h2 id="bmr-math">The BMR math: why any meal-replacement plan can work</h2>

<p>Your body burns energy all day. <strong>BMR</strong> (basal metabolic rate) is what you burn at complete rest. <strong>TDEE</strong> (total daily energy expenditure) adds your movement, exercise, and digestion. Weight loss happens when you eat less than your TDEE over time.</p>
