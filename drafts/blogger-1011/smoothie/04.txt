<p><strong>Step 1: BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor, women)</strong><br>
10 × 70 + 6.25 × 165 − 5 × 35 − 161<br>
= 700 + 1,031 − 175 − 161 = <strong>about 1,395 kcal/day</strong></p>
<p><strong>Step 2: TDEE (lightly active, × 1.375)</strong><br>
1,395 × 1.375 = <strong>about 1,920 kcal/day</strong></p>
<p><strong>Step 3: the deficit</strong><br>
If replacing two meals with smoothies brings her total to about 1,450 kcal, her deficit is roughly <strong>470 kcal/day</strong>.</p>
