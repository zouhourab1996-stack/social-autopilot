<li><strong>Water and glycogen.</strong> When you eat less, especially fewer carbs, your body uses stored glycogen, which is held with water. The first week's drop often includes a lot of water, not only fat.</li>
<li><strong>Less food in your gut.</strong> Lighter meals mean less food weight in your digestive system.</li>
<li><strong>The 3,500-calorie rule overpromises.</strong> Researchers at the NIH showed that the classic "3,500 kcal = 1 pound" rule ignores how your body adapts as you lose weight, so it overestimates long-term loss (Hall et al., <em>The Lancet</em>, 2011).</li>
</ul>
