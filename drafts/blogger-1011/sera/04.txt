<h3>Is the reading really personal?</h3>

<p>The marketing leans on a story of a grandmother's tradition and hand-crafted readings. We cannot verify that story. What we could see is that the public reading page appears to assemble its text from pre-written passages matched to your chosen cards, your zodiac sign, and your focus area, and then lets you download it as a PDF.</p>

<p>That is not a scam in itself. Many tarot apps work the same way. But it does mean two people who pick the same cards, sign, and focus will likely receive very similar text. If you want something written just for you, a live reader is a better fit.</p>
