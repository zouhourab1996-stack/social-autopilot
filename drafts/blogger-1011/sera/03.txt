<li><strong>Best for:</strong> people who enjoy tarot as a reflective ritual and are fine paying for a beautifully presented, mostly pre-written reading.</li>
<li><strong>Skip it if:</strong> you want a live reader, or you hope a reading will solve a real money, health, or relationship problem.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 id="how">How Seraphina Tarot works, step by step</h2>

<p>The funnel is designed to feel like a personal session. Here is the flow we saw on the public pages:</p>

<ol>
<li><strong>Pick three cards</strong> for your Past, Present, and Future from a face-down spread.</li>
<li><strong>Enter your first name</strong>, then your <strong>email</strong> ("where should I send your complete reading?"). An optional box asks for your phone number for text alerts.</li>
<li><strong>Enter your birth date.</strong> The page shows your zodiac sign.</li>
<li><strong>Choose a focus:</strong> Love &amp; Relationships, Money &amp; Abundance, Purpose &amp; Direction, or Health &amp; Energy.</li>
<li>A short "reading room" chat-style page builds anticipation, then you reach the <strong>sales page</strong> with the price.</li>
</ol>

<p>Good to know: you share your name, email, and birthday before you ever see a price. That is common for this kind of offer, but it means you will likely get marketing emails even if you do not buy.</p>
