<h2 id="types">Pick the right kind of video editor</h2>

<p>"Video editor" covers very different skills. Searching for the exact job you need saves a lot of back-and-forth.</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Short-form editors</strong> focus on Reels, TikTok, and Shorts: fast cuts, animated captions, trending pacing.</li>
<li><strong>YouTube editors</strong> handle long talking-head videos, retention edits, B-roll, chapters, and sometimes thumbnails.</li>
<li><strong>Podcast editors</strong> sync multiple cameras, clean audio, and cut clips from long episodes.</li>
