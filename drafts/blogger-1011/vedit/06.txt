<li><strong>Asking to pay or talk outside Fiverr.</strong> Fiverr's terms say payments must go through the site. Moving off-platform removes your protection.</li>
<li><strong>A portfolio full of stock reels</strong> with no client work, or samples that look identical across many different sellers.</li>
<li><strong>Vague answers</strong> about revisions, delivery time, or what "unlimited" really means.</li>
<li><strong>Prices far below the market</strong> for long, complex edits. A 20-minute polished video for $10 usually means corners are cut somewhere.</li>
<li><strong>No questions about your project.</strong> Good editors ask about style, audience, and deadline before they start.</li>
<li><strong>Unclear music rights.</strong> Ask where music comes from. Unlicensed tracks can get your video muted or claimed.</li>
</ul>
