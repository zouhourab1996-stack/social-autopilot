<h2 id="cost">How much does a video editor cost on Fiverr in 2026?</h2>

<p>Every seller sets their own price, so there is no fixed rate. Still, a few patterns hold. In its <a href="https://www.fiverr.com/resources/guides/video-animation/video-editor-cost" target="_blank" rel="noopener">video editor cost guide</a>, Fiverr says a basic editing job (cutting scenes, rearranging, adding transitions) averages about $65, and that editors often price per minute of finished video or per project.</p>

<p>Here is a realistic way to think about budget. These are rough ranges, not quotes, and they move with length, footage quality, and how fast you need it.</p>
